Before you get started

A few questions, answered.

What if I do not know which offer I need?

Start a project inquiry and describe the result you want. The discovery call helps identify whether you need a build, hands-on starting support or an assessment first.

Do I need a team or an established business?

No. BSS works with people developing their first idea, independent business owners, brick-and-mortar businesses and organizations.

Is this advice or done-for-you implementation?

It depends on the offer. The Social Media Growth Plan gives you a strategy to implement. Idea-to-Implementation combines hands-on guidance with agreed setup. Digital builds and systems projects deliver the scoped asset or working setup. Assessments deliver findings and recommendations.

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. Your 30-minute discovery call is free. It is a conversation about fit, scope and next steps. Paid deliverables and timelines are agreed separately.

Can BSS support an existing setup?

Yes. We can explore improvements to an existing setup or ongoing maintenance. Compatibility, access and scope are reviewed before work is agreed.
