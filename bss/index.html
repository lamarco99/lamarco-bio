A few things to know

What if I’m not sure what I need?

Choose “Bring an idea to life” or “Solve a business problem.” Share what you have in mind—even if you don’t know which tools or deliverables you need yet.

Do I need an existing business or a team?

No. BSS works with people developing an idea, solo business owners, and established organizations. The scope is built around your objective.

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. It’s an initial conversation to understand your project and determine possible next steps. Implementation and ongoing support are scoped and priced separately.

Can we work together remotely?

Yes. BSS is based in Chicago and supports local and remote projects.
