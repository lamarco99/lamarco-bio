Before you get started

A few questions, answered.

What if I do not know which offer I need?

Describe the work you repeat and the result you want. On the discovery call, we’ll identify whether a focused assessment or implementation is the right starting point.

Do I need a team or an established business?

You can be a solo entrepreneur, a business owner or an organization. The best starting point is a real task or process you want to improve with AI and automation.

Is this advice or done-for-you implementation?

AI Integration & Workflow Automation delivers the scoped working setup, testing and handoff. The $250 Business Systems Assessment delivers audit findings and recommendations; implementation is separate. Digital builds deliver the agreed asset or setup.

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. Your 30-minute discovery call is free. It is a conversation about fit, scope and next steps. Paid deliverables and timelines are agreed separately.

Can BSS support an existing setup?

Yes. Compatibility, access and scope are reviewed first. For AI implementation clients, Systems Care & Maintenance is an optional add-on for ongoing support; it is discussed with your implementation.
