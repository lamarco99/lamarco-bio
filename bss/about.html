Why I built BSS

The tools are there.
The connection is what’s missing.

Running my own business meant managing inquiries, client information, content, scheduling and delivery. That work pushed me to explore AI tools and build better ways to organize and connect the moving pieces.

Through my own ventures, including SOLIFIC, I’ve worked with AI-assisted content and business setup, client portals, intake and onboarding processes, follow-up workflows and operational dashboards.

BSS brings that experience to business owners who know AI could help but need someone to understand their process, choose a useful starting point and build the agreed solution.
